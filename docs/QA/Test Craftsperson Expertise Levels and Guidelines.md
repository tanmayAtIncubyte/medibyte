# Test Craftsperson Expertise Levels and Guidelines

## **Skill Categories**

### Quality-Driven Thinking

Embodying Product Excellence through Quality-Driven Passion

**Level 1**

- Understands basic testing practices and client requirements.
- Possesses attention to detail and can differentiate between expected and actual results.
- Identifies and reports bugs with some level of help.

**Level 2**

- Independently works on client projects, collaborating closely with clients to test requirements.
- Considers the end-user's perspective when conducting tests.
- Proficient in analyzing and categorizing tickets/bugs based on their severity.
- Ensures rigorous verification and validation of applications.
- Exhibits an exceptional grasp of testing intricacies and methodologies including non-functional elements, encompassing performance, security and usability testing.
- Pairs with developers while they are developing to identify test cases earlier in the SDLC.
- Performs Proof of Concepts(PoCs).

**Level 3**

- Ensures consistent and intuitive quality across all product features.
- Oversees comprehensive testing throughout the development cycle.
- Serves as a go-to person for simple solutions and guidance.
- Suggest soluions based on testing gaps and takes those solutions to closure.
- Establishes best practices within teams and mentors other Test Craftspersons.
- Collaborates with Product Manager to identify test scenarios during requirements gathering.
- Actively seeks frequent feedback from clients to continually improve testing strategies.

**Level 4**

- Strives to improve the quality of products across multiple teams.
- Advocates best practices in quality assurance, promoting a culture of quality across Incubyte.
- Regularly shares knowledge about the importance quality thinking across Incubyte through Lightning talks or blogs.

**Level 5**

- Actively shares knowledge about quality thinking and testing best practices with the broader community.
- Hunts bugs and finds issues in third-party or external applications(Beta Testing) to promote quality thinking.

### Business Domain Knowledge

Combining Quality with Expertise

**Level 1**

- Puts effort in understanding the product features and customer STLC.
- Grasps the concepts and terminology relevant to the business domain.
- Shadows senior QA in designing Test Scenarios, Test Cases, Bug Reports, and Test Summary Reports.

**Level 2**

- Possesses in-depth understanding of client's norms & practices.
- Shows a solid understanding of the features under test.
- Designs Test Cases, creating effective and comprehensive Test Scenarios, Test Plans, Test Metrics, and Test Reports.
- Delve deeper into specific workflows or areas within the business domain.
- Familiar with client/government regulations, industry standards, and privacy policies.

**Level 3**

- Demonstrates exceptional knowledge of the application under test.
- Leads Test Planning activities, including defining Test Objectives, strategies, process improvements and timelines.
- Posesses crucial business knowledge to make well-informed strategic decisions.
- Exhibits such a deep understanding of the domain that they can proactively propose test cases to clients.

**Level 4**

- Experienced in collaborating across various domains across Incubyte and recognized as an expert in at least one.

**Level 5**

- Stays at the forefront of industry trends, participates in conferences and forums in their respective domain.

### Tooling and Technology

Frequently used tools by Test Craftsperson, including tooling that enables working efficiently in an Agile, BDD environment

**Level 1**

- Educates oneself about the needs and uses of the toolchains like issue tracking and management, product documentation, etc.
- Selects a tool from the toolchains and learns to use it in the project context.
- Possesses fundamental knowledge of terminal commands.
- Efficient in using the current platform and environment( OS,shell script, IDE etc.).
- Understands the basics of source control and can pair with developers to create branches and pull requests.
- Skillfully uses documentation tools to maintain comprehensive, yet concise test documentation.

**Level 2**

- Competently utilizes Issue Management Tools.
- Automates and thoroughly documents recurring tasks, enhancing operational efficiency for both individual and team productivity.
- Comfortable writing feature specifications preferably using BDD format.
- Adaptable and adept at various tools efficiently, with a knack for quick tool acquisition.
- Familiar with DevOps and its tools.
- Proficient in Git operations such as reset, merge, rebase, and experienced in navigating the terminal.
- Devotes efforts to automating test scenarios through automation frameworks and follows clean code practices.

**Level 3**

- Attains expertise in development tools at such a level that they can provide guidance to team members in achieving a similar level of expertise.
- Demonstrates a grasp of CI/CD principles and is capable of troubleshooting minor issues within pipeline runs.
- Proficient with IDE features, plugins and extensions, capable of advanced configurations for heightened efficiency and productivity.
- Goes beyond the confines of the automation framework and engages with developers in debugging activities as well.
- Architectures test automation frameworks and recommends solutions, ensuring their successful implementation.
- Determine the most appropriate automation technology stack.

**Level 4**

- Guides other team across Incubyte in selecting an appropriate toolchain in accordance with their project/client.
- Provides support to Test Craftsperson for testing-related matters and in developing scalable and maintainable test automation architectures involving other technologies(such as Kubernetes, Docker, DevOps).
- Possesses knowledge of various automation technologies and suggest techstacks based on that.
- Guides test craftsperson across Incubyte .

**Level 5**

- Widely recognized expert and consistently contributes to the chosen technology or field of interest.
- Represents the Incubyte toolchain and practices by writing and disseminating knowledge about it in the community.
- Contributes to open source automation libraries or frameworks.

### Processes

Agile and lean development processes for a successful client engagement, internal Incubyte processes that ensure client and team happiness

**Level 1**

- Understands the need for and the value proposition of having agile ceremonies and actively participates in all.
- Takes extensive notes while attending meetings to retrospect and improve.
- Takes the initiative instead of waiting to be led.
- Upholds craftsmanship practices even under timeline pressure, with timelines accommodating Incubyte's valued practices.

**Level 2**

- Contributes to the refinement of backlog in testing efforts.
- Validates whether a developed feature aligns with documented requirements and collaborates with the development team to ensure alignment.
- Aware of various test metrics and understands their value in client engagements.
- Provides feedback on testing-related processes based on personal experiences and identified gaps.
- Identifies ways to improve testing-related metrics.
- Delivers incremental value and solicits feedback.
- Provides feedback on client-related processes based on what's best for client engagement.

**Level 3**

- Contributes to improving established processes to ensure client satisfaction
- Leads processes for their project, such as test planning, test prioritization and providing guidance on time estimation.
- Recognizes when process improvements should take priority over new requirements.
- Understands when automation is required and conveys when it's not feasible.
- Adapts to process changes as demanded by the situation, ensuring a prompt return to normalcy.

**Level 4**

- Considers process improvement as an entity that can be enhanced, seeking substantial improvements that impact future projects, client happiness, team satisfaction, and revenue.
- Guides others on test management and test life cycle throughout Incubyte.
- Proposes innovative ideas to enhance team and organizational productivity and continually strives to implement and refine them.

**Level 5**

- Represents successful Incubyte process improvement ideas with broader community.
- Volunteers time to work on technology initiatives benefiting communities.
- Extends invitations to domain experts to actively share widely adopted processes.

### Communication

Internal and external communication

**Level 1**

- Actively participates in all internal sessions within the team.
- Actively participates, collaborates, and engages in all internal and external sessions, ensuring video presence.
- Cultivates a culture of open communication, mutual respect, and honesty.
- Conducts enough self-research before seeking timely help when faced with challenges.
- Communicates at the right level of abstraction being mindful about the use of technical, domain and client specific jargons.
- Respects personal boundaries around race, gender, caste, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, geographical location, and practices inclusivity in all communications.
- Consistently ensures the closure of open communication channels.
- Communicates softly and is easy to approach and collaborate with.

**Level 2**

- Regularly provides valuable contributions in collaborative sessions within the team like RCAs and retrospectives.
- Collaborates effectively with stakeholders to keep moving things forward.
- Establishes rapport and builds trust with clients, enabling independent handling of client engagements.
- Writes comprehensive commit messages and pull request (PR) descriptions that explain not just what has changed but also why.
- Communicates technical concepts thoroughly and effectively to non-technical stakeholders.
- Recognizes when to escalate or highlight blockers or circumstances that could potentially hinder project progress or delivery.

**Level 3**

- Leads efficient and productive standups and other internal/external meetings.
- Pushes back on client requests when they would negatively impact the project.
- Rapidly fosters trust with clients, including new client stakeholders.
- Responds to client escalations, outages or issues promptly and ensures follow-through for resolution.
- Effectively handles challenging communication with both clients and internal teams, and is trusted to independently manage client interactions.
- Facilitates client alignment with our engineering practices and culture.
- Actively contributes to enhancing communication within the team.
- Determines the most effective communication methods with all stakeholders, ensuring timely answers and issue resolutions.
- Proficiently conveys concise and clear reports on project status, product performance, and roadmap to both internal and client stakeholders.
- Skillfully resolves conflicts as they arise and adeptly aligns all stakeholders and teams on the same page.

**Level 4**

- Functions as a coach to the Product and Quality Guild.
- Help others in communicating with clients and provides advice in that regard.
- Proactively addresses and deescalates tense situations, demonstrating readiness to engage in challenging conversations with clients.
- Bridges the gap between the members of the Product and Quality Guild
- Contributes to improving communication processes within the company.
- Help in onboarding new clients and takes ownership of all client communication.
- Exhibits a keen understanding of the psychological and behavioral aspects of communication and their impact on both individuals and teams.

**Level 5**

- Proficiently conducts discussions and meetings at the founder/CEO level, making valuable contributions to Incubyte's client acquisition efforts.
- Earns respect as an approachable and helpful advisor in their area of specialization.
- Invites domain experts and hosts the software craftmanship talks.

### Feedback and Mentoring

Helping others in your team and at Incubyte to get better

**Level 1**

- Clearly defines what they want to achieve from their mentors and is committed to putting in the effort to achieve them.
- Actively seeks early feedback on their work.
- Demonstrates a learning-oriented mindset and receives feedback in a constructive and positive manner.
- Effortlessly engages in pair programming.

**Level 2**

- Offers impactful and influential feedback to fellow team members.
- Delivers constructive criticism with finesse and in a thoroughly professional manner.
- Identifies opportunities for improvement and collaboratively initiates change with the support of fellow team members.
- Ensures every team member is held accountable for delivering the highest quality of work.
- Actively contributes while pairing in different team and peer to peer activities with colleagues at different levels of experience and expertise.

**Level 3**

- Gauges a team member's grasp of a specific technology, domain or module and offers guidance to enhance their proficiency in project-related tasks.
- Help new recruits, offering guidance and support in overcoming obstacles and addressing issues encountered in their tasks.
- They conduct technical or domain-specific "brown bag" sessions.
- Educates a team member through collaborative pairing.

**Level 4**

- Offers improvement-oriented feedback to every Craftsperson, regardless of their domain expertise.
- Invests in working with team members, helping them identify areas for growth and personally help them in advancing to the next level of expertise.
- Escalates performance issues of team members to the right people in a timely manner.
- Demonstrates empathy towards colleagues by actively listening, understanding their concerns, and taking proactive steps to address and resolve those issues.

**Level 5**

- Acts as a role model and mentor to other mentors and to a broader community.
- Serves as a mentor to our customers.
- Works to improve mentoring processes across the company.

Help establish our brand with a broader community, hire peers you’d love to work with, and refine internal processes.

- A 1 means that you identify as an Incubee on public forums.
- A 3 means that you actively contribute to Incubyte's growth.
- A 5 means that you enhance Incubyte's brand with your own personal brand.

**Level 1**

- Associates with Incubyte on public forums and most social portals.
- Likes, reposts, and spreads Incubyte news.
- Maintains and regularly updates social media profiles with Incubyte’s latest activities and announcements.

**Level 2**

- Finds and offers references for best-matching candidates for open roles.
- Participates in assessing candidates via submissions and interviews.
- Helps identify hiring events where Incubyte could participate.
- Helps identify speaking opportunities for Incubyte at local and global conferences.
- Actively participates in internal initiatives and discussions.
- Provides consistent feedback to help improve internal processes.

**Level 3**

- Helps Incubyte create content helpful to prospective clients and employees (e.g., case studies, blogs, videos, technology-related articles, new ways of working, etc.).
- Helps in interviews and expedites the process of finding suitable candidates.
- Understands Incubyte beyond the assigned project or team.
- Works on improving internal processes
- Works with People Success and Operations teams to understand current challenges and help solve them.
- Leads trainings and is actively involved in growth of other Incubees
- Takes up tasks outside of project work that help improve Incubyte’s internal processes.

**Level 4**

- Advocates Incubyte’s methodologies, technologies, and values to the wider community, via Talks, sessions or other mediums
- Assists in sales and pre-sales processes.
- Looks for opportunities to expand client and team relationships.
- Collaborates closely across teams to establish consistency in ways of working, utilizing personal subject matter expertise.
- Has made contributions that have led to significant impact at Incubyte

**Level 5**

- Gives talks on the Incubyte YouTube channel.
- Actively considers hiring as its own entity and helps improve processes to hire faster and better.
- Contributes to learning, development, and training initiatives.

### Learning, Sharing & Community

Navigating personal learning paths and actively sharing knowledge internally at Incubyte and with the broader community

**Level 1**

- Regularly attends meetups and community events.
- Reads posts and newsletters from the software and testing community, and shares them with others for discussion.
- Takes ownership of personal learning journeys.

**Level 2**

**Level 4**

- Regularly conducts knowledge-sharing sessions within and across Incubyte.
- Occasionally conducts meetups, virtual events, or other knowledge-sharing sessions with the broader community.
